What We Do

Through tailored consulting services, EECG empowers organizations to build leadership pipelines, improve employee satisfaction, and create sustainable frameworks that drive organizational success. Programs

Owning Your Future

14-week pipeline program

A career and entrepreneurship pipeline for high school and early college students

"Owning Your Future" is a 14-week professional development program designed to bridge the gap between high school and early college students, preparing them for both immediate employment and long-term entrepreneurial success. The curriculum is built to align with state career and technical education standards.

Delivery includes weekly guest speakers from industry, field trips to local businesses, banks and incubators, hands-on projects, and a capstone in which every student develops a full business plan with financial projections and marketing strategy.

Objective 01

Financial literacy: budgeting, investing, taxes, and credit management

Objective 02

Career readiness: practical workforce skills and professionalism

Objective 03

Business management: operations, franchising, and startup fundamentals

Objective 04

Entrepreneurial thinking: innovation, ownership, and independence

Objective 05

Networking and mentorship with entrepreneurs and industry leaders
